*Question*  
Is clinician use of generative artificial intelligence–drafted responses to patient inbox messages associated with reduced message turnaround time and clinician messaging burden?

*Findings*   
In this cohort study of 983 154 patient portal message responses from 1542 clinicians and 272 091 patients, AI-based response tool adoption was associated with an 8.6% increase in turnaround time, while response tool use was associated with a 29.5% reduction. Response tool use was not associated with an overall reduction in after-hours responding behavior, although associations varied by message type and when the message was received.

*Meaning*     
These findings suggest that clinician use of an AI-based response tool was associated with reduced overall message turnaround time but no overall reduction of after-hours burden.
